Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jacob Kappler ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website.

Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect personal information through accounts, registration forms, analytics services, advertising trackers, or similar tracking technologies on this website.

If you choose to contact us by email, we may receive your email address and any information you voluntarily include in your message. We use this information to respond to your inquiry, provide support, or otherwise communicate with you about your request.

How We Use Information

Information you voluntarily provide to us through email is used for purposes related to responding to your message, providing support, communicating with you, or addressing your request.

We do not use information submitted through email for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Website Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. As part of providing and securing its services, GitHub may process technical information associated with visits to websites hosted through GitHub Pages, which may include information such as IP addresses, device information, request times, and website usage information.

GitHub's processing of information is governed by GitHub's own privacy practices. For more information, please review GitHub's Privacy Statement .

Cookies and Tracking

We do not intentionally use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or other tracking technologies on this website.

Third-party services used to host or provide resources for this website may use their own technologies in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Third-Party Services and Links

This website contains links to third-party websites and services, including the Apple App Store and social media platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, GitHub, and Product Hunt.

These links take you to websites operated by third parties. Once you leave this website or interact with a third-party service, that service's own privacy policy and practices apply. We do not control how those third parties collect, use, or process information.

Font Awesome

This website currently uses Font Awesome icons provided through the cdnjs content delivery network. When a page containing these icons is loaded, your browser may request the required files from the cdnjs service operated by Cloudflare.

This third-party request is used to load website assets and display icons. We do not use Font Awesome or cdnjs for analytics, advertising, or user tracking.

Email

Our website may provide links using the mailto: protocol. These links open your device's or browser's configured email application. We do not receive information simply because you click an email link.

If you choose to send us an email, we will receive the information contained in that email, including your email address and any information you choose to provide.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to respond to your request, operate our services, comply with applicable law, protect our rights, or protect the security of our services.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information that is voluntarily provided to us. However, no method of electronic communication or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please do not send sensitive personal information, such as passwords, financial information, or government identification numbers, by email.

Data Retention

Email communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide support, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through this website.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website or our privacy practices. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, you can Email Us
